# Materials

Keep the ingredients for your next upgrade close at hand.

| Material | Early source | Common use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Planks | Craft logs | Crafting tables, sticks, wooden tools |
| Cobblestone | Mine stone with a pickaxe | Stone tools and furnaces |
| Coal | Mine coal ore | Torches and furnace fuel |
| Charcoal | Smelt logs or wood | An alternative for torches and fuel |
| Iron ingot | Smelt raw iron | Iron tools, buckets, shields |
| Diamond | Mine diamond ore with iron or better | Diamond tools and equipment |

!!! tip "A small reserve goes a long way"
    Keep spare logs, coal or charcoal, and iron near your work area. They cover many routine crafting tasks.
