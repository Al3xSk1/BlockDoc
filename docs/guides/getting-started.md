# Your first day

**Goal:** reach your first night with basic tools, food, and a sheltered work area.

This walkthrough targets ordinary vanilla survival in Java and Bedrock 1.21. Adapt the order to your spawn point and difficulty.

## Gather a starter kit

- [ ] Collect a few logs.
- [ ] Craft planks and a crafting table.
- [ ] Craft sticks and a wooden pickaxe.
- [ ] Mine enough cobblestone for a stone pickaxe and a furnace.
- [ ] Collect food and look for coal, or prepare charcoal.

Use the [recipe reference](../recipes/index.md) when you need a crafting pattern.

## Upgrade early

Switch to a [[items/tools/pickaxes|stone pickaxe]] as soon as you can. Keep some wood for handles, torches, and extra crafting supplies.

!!! tip "Charcoal is a useful backup"
    Smelt a log in a furnace to produce charcoal. It can replace coal when crafting torches.

## Before dark

1. Choose a small shelter location.
2. Close exposed entrances and light the interior.
3. Place your crafting table and furnace inside.
4. Cook food and store spare materials.
5. Craft a bed if you have the materials.

??? note "When a bed is available"
    A bed needs three matching wool blocks and three planks. Place it somewhere safe with enough space to use it; sleeping rules also depend on nearby monsters and multiplayer settings.

## Next steps

Gather iron, expand your storage, and plan a small [[blocks/building/index|building palette]]. Return to the [reference hub](../reference/index.md) whenever you need a quick lookup.
