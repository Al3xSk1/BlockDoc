## Before the first sunset

A small plan makes a new world feel like home.

- [ ] Gather logs and craft a workbench.
- [ ] Make a pickaxe and collect cobblestone.
- [ ] Upgrade to stone tools and build a furnace.
- [ ] Find food and make a few torches.
- [ ] Settle into a lit shelter before nightfall.

Coal is useful, but charcoal will do. Smelt a log when you need fuel for your first torches.

## Notes in the margin

**Choose the right pickaxe.** A golden tool can be quick, but it cannot harvest diamond ore. [Compare mining tiers](items/tools/pickaxes.md).

**Keep some stone as stone.** Silk Touch preserves the block; a furnace turns cobblestone back into stone. [Read the stone entry](blocks/natural/stone.md).

**Refresh a fading signal.** Redstone dust loses strength along a line. A correctly placed repeater gives it a fresh start. [Follow the signal](mechanics/redstone/signal-strength.md).

*Leave room for the next discovery. There is always another page.*
